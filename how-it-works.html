The Process

How It Works

From your first conversation to receiving funds, here is what the business funding process looks like with Estrada Capital Group.

Submit Your Information

Start by completing our brief application form with your basic business details. We ask for information like your business name, industry, monthly revenue, time in business, and how much funding you are looking for. The entire process takes just a few minutes.

You can submit your information through our online form, or call us directly at (559) 737-0273 to provide your details over the phone.

Review Your Funding Options

Based on the information you provide, a funding specialist will review your business profile and identify the funding products you may qualify for. We look at your full picture, including your revenue, time in business, and what you need the capital for, to match you with the right options.

This is not a one-size-fits-all process. We present multiple options when available so you can compare and make an informed decision.

Speak with a Funding Specialist

You will have a focused conversation with a funding specialist who will walk you through your available options, answer your questions, and explain the terms, rates, and repayment structure of each product. There is no pressure to commit during this conversation.

This is your opportunity to ask about anything you want to understand better, including total cost of capital, repayment timelines, and how each option fits your specific situation.

Choose Your Option

Once you have reviewed the options and asked your questions, you choose the funding product that best fits your business goals and budget. We walk you through the final paperwork and make sure you understand every detail before you move forward.

Receive Funding

After your application is approved and all required documentation is complete, funding is deposited into your business bank account. Timelines vary depending on the specific product and the completeness of your documentation.

Important: All funding is subject to approval based on the provider's underwriting criteria. Submission of an application does not guarantee approval or specific funding amounts. Timelines vary by product and individual circumstances.

Be Prepared

What You Will Typically Need

While requirements vary by funding product, having the following information ready can help move the process forward efficiently.

  • Business entity documents (LLC, Corp, etc.)
  • Employer Identification Number (EIN)
  • Business bank account statements (recent 3-6 months)
  • Basic information about your monthly revenue
  • Description of how you plan to use the funds
  • Personal identification
