Understanding Your Options

What Is Business Funding?

Business funding refers to the capital that companies use to cover their operating costs, invest in growth opportunities, purchase equipment, manage cash flow, or handle unexpected expenses. Unlike personal loans, business funding is structured around the financial health and revenue of your company.

There are many types of business funding available, from traditional SBA loans and lines of credit to newer solutions like merchant cash advances and revenue-based financing. The right funding option depends on your specific business situation, including how long you have been operating, your monthly revenue, and what you plan to use the capital for.

At Estrada Capital Group, we work with business owners to identify the funding solutions that align with their goals and qualifications, rather than trying to fit every business into the same product.

The Value of Capital

How Business Funding Supports Growth

Access to the right capital at the right time can make the difference between stalling out and scaling up. Here is how funding helps businesses move forward.

Manage Cash Flow

Many businesses experience gaps between when expenses are due and when revenue comes in. Funding can bridge those gaps and keep operations running without interruption.

Invest in Growth

Whether it is opening a new location, expanding your team, or launching a new product line, growth requires capital. The right funding gives you the resources to take the next step.

Build Stability

Having access to capital creates a financial cushion that helps your business handle unexpected expenses, market changes, and seasonal fluctuations with less disruption.
